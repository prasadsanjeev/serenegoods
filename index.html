Do you build websites for businesses in Durham Region?

Yes. I’m a web designer and developer based in Durham Region, Ontario, building websites for businesses in Ajax, Pickering, Whitby, Oshawa, Clarington and across the GTA. I also work remotely with clients anywhere.

What kinds of websites do you build?

Business websites, portfolios and personal sites, landing pages, and redesigns, as well as custom systems such as a real estate CRM, event ticketing, and online tools.

What are SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) helps your site rank in Google search. AEO (answer engine optimization) structures your content so Google’s answer boxes and voice assistants can quote it directly. GEO (generative engine optimization) helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity understand, recommend and cite your business.

Will my new website show up on Google?

Every site I build includes the foundations search engines look for: clean structure, fast pages, metadata, sitemaps, and structured data. Rankings also depend on your competition and content, so no one can honestly guarantee a position, but your site will be built to be found.

Can you improve the SEO of my existing website?

Yes. I can review an existing website and improve its search and AI visibility, or redesign it from the ground up if that serves you better.
